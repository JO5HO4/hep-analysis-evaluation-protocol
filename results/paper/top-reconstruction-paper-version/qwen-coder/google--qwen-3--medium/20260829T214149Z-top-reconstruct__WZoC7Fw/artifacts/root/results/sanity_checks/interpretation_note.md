# Mass Distribution Sanity Check

The invariant mass distribution of selected top candidates shows that truth-matched triplets have a mean mass of 166.10 GeV with a standard deviation of 30.72 GeV. This is reasonably close to the expected top quark mass of 172.5 GeV, with the difference likely due to detector resolution effects, reconstruction biases, and the specific selection criteria. The width is physically plausible for reconstructed hadronic top decays.

In contrast, fake triplets (random jet combinations) have a higher mean mass of 236.58 GeV with a much larger width of 138.90 GeV, which is expected as they represent random combinations of jets without the kinematic constraints of a real top decay.

The mass distribution for truth-matched triplets is physically plausible and shows that the reconstruction pipeline is selecting candidates with appropriate kinematic properties.
