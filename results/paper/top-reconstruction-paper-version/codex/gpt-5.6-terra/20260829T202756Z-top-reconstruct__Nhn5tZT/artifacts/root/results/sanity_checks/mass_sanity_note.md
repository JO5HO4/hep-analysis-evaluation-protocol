The selected mass spectrum is plotted against independent validation truth and fake candidates in `../select_triplets/plots/mass_comparison.png`. The selected distribution has median 173.1 GeV and a 5--95% interval of 130.7--400.7 GeV. Its broad finite width is physically expected from generator-jet clustering and residual combinatorial candidates; no mass-window fit or mass-constraining transformation was used, so it is not an artificially narrow spike.
