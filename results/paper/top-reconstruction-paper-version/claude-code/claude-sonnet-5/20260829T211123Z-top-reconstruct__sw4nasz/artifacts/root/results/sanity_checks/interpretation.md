# Mass sanity-check interpretation

- Selected top-candidate mass distribution: mean=187.2 GeV, std=57.9 GeV (PDG top mass = 172.8 GeV).
- Truth-matched candidates within the selection peak near the top mass (mean=167.2 GeV, std=26.7 GeV), consistent with genuine hadronic top decays reconstructed from generator-level jets (finite jet energy resolution and out-of-cone radiation broaden the peak — it is not an artificially narrow spike).
- Fake (combinatorial) selected candidates have a broader, shifted mass distribution (mean=199.2 GeV, std=67.5 GeV), as expected since they are not built from the three jets of a single decaying top quark.
- The overall selected-candidate distribution is a mixture of the truth-like peak and the broader combinatorial tail (consistent with the ~37% selection purity reported in selection_report.json), rather than a single narrow line -- this is the physically expected shape for a triplet-score-selected sample that is not perfectly pure.
